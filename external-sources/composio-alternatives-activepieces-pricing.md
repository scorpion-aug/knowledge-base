Pricing | Activepieces — "A path for every team at your company to build with AI."

## Free — $0, free forever, no card
- 1,000 credits a month
- Unlimited flows
- Up to 5 users
- Chat to automation
- Flows, Agents, Tables
- MCPs and API access

## Plus — $20 flat per month
- Everything in Free, plus:
- 10× the Free plan (10,000 credits a month)
- Then pay as you go ($0.007/credit)
- Up to 5 users
- Bring your own AI keys

## Team — $200 flat per month
- Everything in Plus, plus:
- 5× the Plus plan (50,000 credits a month)
- Unlimited projects
- 25 users included
- SSO and standard roles
- Global connections
- Email support

## Ultimate — Custom
- Everything in Team, plus:
- Credits sized to you, yearly
- Dedicated workers
- Execution priority
- SCIM and custom roles
- Releases and Git Sync
- Audit logs and secrets
- Dedicated support

## Embed — From $36k a year
- "Put a flow builder, AI agents and 770+ integrations inside your own product, under your own brand."
- White-label builder, one SDK, your pieces and templates

## Enterprise control — Custom
- "Run Activepieces on your own servers, or fully air-gapped, on your security team's terms."
- SSO and SCIM; Custom RBAC; Audit logs; Secret managers
